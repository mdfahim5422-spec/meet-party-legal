User Data Deletion Instructions - Meet Party

If you signed in to Meet Party using Facebook or Google and wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Meet Party app on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
